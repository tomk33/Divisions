Divisions

How to Play

1. Each turn begins with deploying new units to your territories.

2. Attack neighboring territories to expand your empire.

3. Fortify your position by positioning new troops and moving troops between your territories.

4. Eliminate all opponents to win.

Settings

Left Mouse Click : Territory Selection and Information

Left Mouse Drag : Panning/Movement

Right Mouse Drag : Rotation of the Map

Scroll Wheel : Zooming
